Privacy Policy for Mosaquli

Applies to all editions and platforms of Mosaquli: Color Puzzle and Mosaquli: Color Puzzle Pro.
In a nutshell:
Mosaquli is a pure offline color logic puzzle game. It operates no remote servers, collects no personal data outside your device, and never transfers any information to AppsMakerDeluxe Studios or third parties.

1. Scope & Covered Application Editions

This privacy policy applies uniformly to the following Android package identifiers published by AppsMakerDeluxe Studios:

Google Play Free Edition: com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli (Free to play)
HUAWEI AppGallery Free Edition: com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli.huawei (Free to play)
HUAWEI AppGallery Pro Edition: com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli.pro.huawei (Paid one-time download)
Google Play Pro Edition (Future Option): com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli.pro (Planned premium release)

All editions adhere to the exact same privacy-first architecture: complete privacy, zero tracking, and local game execution.

2. Data Controller

The responsible controller for data processing under GDPR and international privacy regulations is:

AppsMakerDeluxe Studios
Developer & Privacy Support: appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

3. Core Principles & Data Minimization

The game strictly follows the principle of Privacy by Design and by Default:

  • No Analytics or Diagnostics: Neither Google Firebase Analytics, Unity Analytics, nor third-party telemetry tools are embedded.
  • 100% Ad-Free: No commercial advertising banners, interstitials, or reward videos are included.
  • No User Account: No registration, login, or personal contact details are requested or stored.
  • No In-App Purchases or Subscriptions: Neither edition features internal in-app microtransactions or recurring subscriptions. The Pro edition is purchased solely as an upfront store download.

4. Local On-Device Data Processing

Mosaquli stores and processes data exclusively inside your device's protected internal app storage (App Sandbox):

5. Store Declarations (Google Play & HUAWEI AppGallery)

Different application stores utilize distinct taxonomies for disclosure:

Google Play Data Safety

In the Google Play Console for com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli (and any future Pro listing), the disclosures state: No data collected and No data shared, since zero data is transmitted to AppsMakerDeluxe or third parties.

HUAWEI AppGallery Privacy Tags

Huawei's privacy taxonomy encompasses functional data processed strictly on the local device. In compliance with this taxonomy, com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli.huawei and com.appsmakerdeluxe.mosaquli.pro.huawei declare:

  • Scenario: App functionality
  • Category User information: Game statistics (locally retaining completed puzzles and stars)
  • Category App information: App settings (locally storing language, audio, and visual preferences)
  • Zero Transmission: No analytics, no advertising/marketing, no third-party disclosure, and no cross-border data transfer.

6. Android Permissions

Mosaquli requires no sensitive runtime permissions:

  • No Network Permission: The app does not request android.permission.INTERNET.
  • Haptic Feedback: Subtle tactile feedback when routing marbles utilizes standard Android view haptic feedback mechanisms without needing the VIBRATE permission.

7. Manual Savegame Export & Pro Migration (Free → Pro)

Mosaquli provides an optional, user-initiated backup and migration feature in the settings menu:

  • Manual Backup: You can export your progress into a structured text file via the Android system share sheet or file picker.
  • Seamless Upgrade to Pro: When upgrading to Mosaquli Pro, you can import this file to immediately unlock your earned stars, completed worlds, and mosaic gallery in the Pro edition.
  • Full User Control: This procedure is never performed automatically in the background. The exported file remains strictly under your control. Before importing, the app performs a rigorous schema and integrity check.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & International Standards)

Because Mosaquli never stores personal information on remote servers, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Data Deletion: You can wipe your game progress at any time directly in the game settings, clear application storage in Android system settings, or uninstall the app. All local game data will be eradicated immediately.
  • Inquiries & Support: For any privacy-related questions or license information, feel free to reach out via email.
